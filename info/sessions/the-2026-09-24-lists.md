## Rejected approaches (do not retry)

- **Fading the whole page for the screen transition.** Every page paints Orbit's ground, so fading the
  page fades that ground and shows the platform window through it - a flash of the wrong colour between
  every two screens. Fade the page's *content*.
- **Keeping the tick box's drawing on the button that now opens the note's tool menu.** A tick box that
  opens a menu is a lie about what the press does; it is the "⋯" and keeps the accent wash for the mode.
- **`grid-template-rows: masonry`** for the task cards. No browser ships it unflagged; multi-column is
  the only pure-CSS packing, at the cost of reading down-then-across.
- **Taking "three empty lines" literally** for the room at the foot of a note. That is the browser's
  measure; on the phone a line is ~18 and the furniture is 128, so the room is the furniture's height.
- **`dotnet ef`/`dotnet test` and an Android build at the same time.** They contend; run them in
  sequence.
